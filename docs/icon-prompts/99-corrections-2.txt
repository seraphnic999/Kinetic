Draw icons in exactly the style of the attached reference image, which is the
approved icon set for this app. Match its line weight, its simplicity and its

This is a REDRAW of one icon that already exists but came back too sparse. The
note says what arrived and what it should be instead - the "not a ..." part
matters as much as the rest.

- Line art only. Every shape is an open stroke with rounded ends. No filled
  shapes, no shading, no double lines.
- Simple and plain. Draw the object, not a detailed illustration of it.
- Draw the icon as large as it will go in its canvas, centred.
- One icon per file.

FILE FORMAT

- SVG, viewBox="0 0 64 64", no width or height attributes
- fill="none", stroke="currentColor", stroke-width="1.5",
  stroke-linecap="round", stroke-linejoin="round"
- Any number of <path>, <circle>, <line>, <polyline> elements
- No transform attributes - bake positions into the coordinates
- No <mask>, <clipPath>, <defs> or <use> - they cannot be folded into a
  single path and the build rejects the file
- Filename is the icon name exactly: emptySessions.svg

DRAW THIS 1:

emptySessions   a squat rack with no barbell on it, seen straight from the
                front. Three things have to be legible:

                1. TWO UPRIGHTS, each standing on a foot that is clearly wider
                   than the post, so the shape reads as a rack standing on the
                   floor rather than as two loose vertical lines.

                2. A J-HOOK near the top of each upright, drawn as a small
                   open U or J cradle that sticks INWARD, toward the other
                   upright. These are the part that says "rack". Draw them
                   big enough to read - an open cradle, not a short stub.

                3. A DASHED HORIZONTAL LINE spanning between the two cradles,
                   at cradle height, showing where the missing bar would sit.
                   This is what makes the icon say EMPTY. Dashed, never solid
                   - a solid line there is just a rack with a bar in it, which
                   is the opposite meaning.

                What arrived: two plain vertical lines with a tiny stub on
                each and a short foot. At 72px it does not resolve into an
                object at all - it reads as two stray brackets. NOT two bare
                verticals, NOT a stub instead of a cradle, NOT a solid bar.

                Sibling icons, so you can pitch the "emptiness" the same way:
                emptyChart is chart axes with a DASHED baseline and no bars.
                Use the same dashed convention here.
